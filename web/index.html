Editing the board

  • Place: click a square to put the selected figure there. Choose the figure under Figure: its colour, its variant (plain, chameleon or mirrored) and its rotation.
  • Erase, Hole, Wall: click a square to empty it, remove it from the board, or block it.
  • Annotate: drag from one square to another for an arrow, or click a square for the circle, cross, square, triangle or dot chosen under Annotation, in the chosen colour. Doing the same again removes it; with another colour selected, it changes colour instead. Click an annotation in the list under Annotation to remove just that one.
  • Drag a figure to move it. Right-click a square to empty it.
  • With the pointer over a square: R rotates the figure 45° clockwise, Shift+R anticlockwise, Del empties the square.
  • On a touch screen, long-press a figure for buttons that rotate it (↺ ↻), change its colour between white, black and neutral, or remove it (✕).

Extended FEN

Standard FEN works unchanged (KQRBNP white, kqrbnp black, digits for empty squares, / between ranks, top rank first). In addition:

{Q90} {n225}piece rotated clockwise, in steps of 45°
{=Q} {=R180}neutral piece (0/90/180/270°)
{~B}chameleon (framed) piece
{!N90}mirrored knight (90/180/270°)
{@Star} {@star} {=@equihopper}special figure: capital = white, lower case = black, = = neutral
.hole: a missing square
*wall: a blocked square
12multi-digit empty runs, for boards up to 26×26

Captions

Captions go above and below the board, left-aligned, and wrap to the diagram's width. Write <b>bold</b> and <i>italic</i>, or select text and press B or I (Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I).

Export

SVG and PDF download the diagram as shown. TeX downloads a snippet to include with the gcsnippet LaTeX package.
